The big story / Fitting room

The front door is working. The fitting room
is losing the sale.

Footfall is up 14.8% and the new window is pulling people in. But the queue behind the curtain peaked at 7 minutes 40 seconds, and 11 shoppers left before they tried anything on.

By the floor desk · 18:42 PKT · Live

At 16:12 the store held 48 people. The window had stopped 46 in the previous hour, the denim table was the busiest square metre on the floor, and the fitting room had two rooms in use. By 16:40, seven people were waiting. Eleven left the queue before their turn.

The store did not have a traffic problem. It had a throughput problem. Capture is running at 19.6%, just under the 20% target, and the window is doing its job. The leak is further in: only 31% of engaged shoppers reached a fitting room, down from 38% on the four-week average.

“We had one person on fitting and a queue of seven. It wasn’t a traffic problem; it was a coverage problem.”Floor lead, 17:20

The cost is visible in the conversion line: 24.1% against a 26.4% baseline, a gap of 2.3 points — 55 purchases at today’s traffic. The queue is not the whole gap, but it is the most fixable part: 11 shoppers left before trying anything on. The fix is not more cameras. It is one associate, in the right place, for two hours.

The fix

Move one associate to fitting, 16:00–18:00. Open the third room. Replenish sizes 28 and 30. Expected: wait below 5:00 and 6–8 recovered try‑ons.
